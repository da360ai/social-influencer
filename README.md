# Influence Income Hub

Build an offline workshop landing page for 'EARN FROM YOUR INFLUENCE - 3-Hour Offline Influencer Income Mastery Workshop' by Digital Academy 360 (ticket price ₹79, regular ₹1999).

Design & Layout Requirements:
1. Hero Section matching the layout and aesthetic from the attached screenshot (attachment 2d9ec80d-bde6-42a6-a1aa-3cca700fe5eb):
   - Dark crimson / deep wine background (#160608, #2a0b10) with subtle grid lines, radial glow highlights, and identical typography (Plus Jakarta Sans / Inter style).
   - Left side:
     - Pill badge: 'OFFLINE SEMINAR — BANGALORE'
     - Main headline: 'Get ready to Earn From Your Influence.' (highlighting 'Earn From Your' or 'Influence' in warm amber/gold or bright accent).
     - Subtitle: 'Understand exactly how creators turn attention into income and build your first monetisation roadmap. Real strategies. Real examples. Real opportunities.'
     - Meta pills: '⌛ 3 HOURS', '📅 UPCOMING SATURDAY', '🕙 10AM TO 1PM', '📍 OFFLINE WORKSHOP'
     - CTA button: 'Claim your spot →' with strike price '₹1999 ₹79 Only'
     - Social proof: '★★★★★ Rated 4.8/5 by 3,730+ learners'
   - Right side registration card:
     - Header: '📍 IN-PERSON · JP NAGAR, BLR' and badge '🔥 5 seats left'
     - 3-step indicator: '1 Fill details', '2 Pay via Razorpay', '3 WhatsApp confirm'
     - Form fields: Full Name, Email Address, WhatsApp Number (+91 prefix)
     - Submit button: 'Book Your Seat Now →' (₹1999 ₹79 Only)
     - Trust footer: Razorpay badge, '100% Secure UPI · VISA · Mastercard · RuPay'
     - Interactive state handling: simulated checkout flow leading to success confirmation modal with ticket details & calendar invite download.

2. Full Landing Page Sections using the same dark crimson theme, typography, and badges:
   - Floating header/sticky bar with Digital Academy 360 branding, countdown timer, and quick 'Book for ₹79' CTA.
   - Core Creator Journey Framework: visual step cards for 'CREATE' (Build content people want to consume) → 'GROW' (Build attention, engagement and audience trust) → 'POSITION' (Become valuable in a specific niche) → 'MONETISE' (Turn attention and trust into income).
   - Full 10-Module Curriculum with expandable or interactive cards:
     1. The Creator Economy Reality Check
     2. Find Your Creator Position
     3. Content That Creates Influence
     4. From Followers to Influence
     5. The "Value Beats Followers"
     6. 6 Ways to Make Money as a Creator
     7. The Creator Income Stack
     8. How to Get Your First Brand Deal
     9. How to Price Your Content
     10. Your First ₹10K Creator Roadmap
   - 2 High-Value Bonuses Showcase with distinct gift badges:
     - Bonus 1: 30 AI Skills & Prompts for Creators (Tools, prompts, workflows you can use immediately)
     - Bonus 2: Creator Earning Career Roadmap (A clear path from content to income)
   - Workshop Outcomes categorized into Strategy, Content, Growth, Monetisation, Business, and Roadmap.
   - Comparison Grid: 'Who Should Attend' (7 creator types) vs 'Who Should NOT Attend' (5 clear warnings with ❌).
   - 6 Tangible Takeaways (Positioning statement, 3 Content pillars, 10 Ideas, Brand pitch template, Pricing framework, Career roadmap).
   - Venue & Experience details (In-Person JP Nagar Bangalore campus, live interactive format).
   - FAQ section answering common questions about timings, recordings, offline venue, prerequisites.
   - Final booking CTA section and sticky mobile booking footer.
